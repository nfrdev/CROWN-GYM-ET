Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining CROWN GYM in Dukem.

Where is CROWN GYM located in Dukem?

CROWN GYM is conveniently located in Dukem, Oromia, Ethiopia, easily accessible from central Dukem and the main road. Message us on WhatsApp at 0999 087 712 for pinpoint directions or to schedule a visit.

What are CROWN GYM's operating hours?

We are open 7 days a week, 12 hours every day across two training sessions: Morning session from 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM, and Evening session from 3:00 PM to 10:00 PM (Monday to Sunday).

How much does gym membership cost?

We offer flexible options: Daily Pass at 150 ETB, Monthly Plan at 1,500 ETB (only 50 ETB/day), 3 Months at 4,000 ETB (save 500 ETB), and 6 Months at 7,500 ETB (save 1,500 ETB - our best value at 1,250 ETB/mo).

Is CROWN GYM beginner-friendly?

Yes, absolutely! We welcome members of all fitness levels. Our environment is welcoming, encouraging, and our staff provides beginner equipment orientation to help you start your journey safely and confidently.

What workout equipment and amenities are available?

CROWN GYM is equipped with full-range free weights (dumbbells, barbells, weight plates), power racks, bench press stations, resistance machines, a dedicated cardio training zone (treadmills, ellipticals), as well as secure lockers and changing rooms.
